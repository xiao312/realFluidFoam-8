# Nonideal diffusion driving-force sensitivity

This draft tests an additional SRK composition driving force without changing
the declared Liang baseline. It does not establish the paper's undocumented
flux equation. Its numerical verdict and its effect on the Case 4-to-5 gap
must be reported separately.

Source: `8d5d6405f35c256e59b334b50bc40f8b558ef832`, branch
`draft/case5-diffusion`. SDK: pinned Cantera 3.2 build 124917358. Build 125045036
completed in 1 min 8 s. Kernel, alpha and Takahashi checks passed. The new
actual Flow1D test passed all 12 ideal/Case 4/Case 5 face comparisons, including
zero net diffusive mass flux and identical ideal flow. Mixed diagnostic options
are rejected. These checks qualify the implemented sensitivity, not a flame speed.

The added uncorrected species mass flux is
`-rho * (W_i/Wbar) * D_i * X_i * d(log(phi_i))/dx`, with the composition
derivative evaluated at constant midpoint T and p. The usual mass correction
is applied to the additional flux. The stock energy equation, chemistry and
transport coefficients remain unchanged. No Soret term is added.
The implementation follows the frozen diagnostic described in the
[diffusion and inversion record](liang-diffusion-and-inversion-20261020.md).
Giovangigli et al. (2011), Section 4.2, motivates this driving force, but uses
a more complete transport formulation. It is not evidence of Liang's closure.
