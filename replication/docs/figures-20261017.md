# Figure comparison, 2026-10-17

All 27 numbered figures in the supplied 2022 and 2025 papers are included in a
local 28-page PDF and offline HTML atlas. Six figures have independently computed
plots: two property comparisons and four startup diagnostics. Eight numerical
figures lack results; twelve diagrams and one reference map remain source context.
No scientific replication result is accepted.

## Observations

The 2022 Figure 6 methane SRK curves closely overlay our calculations. Across
19 resolvable pressure/property series, RMS relative differences against the
approximately digitized PDF vectors range from 0.013% to 0.355%. The 1-atm density
curve is too compressed on the printed scale to score. The clipped 50-atm Cp peak
is excluded, with no interpolation across the gap. These errors include drawing
precision and interpolation; they are not pre-agreed acceptance tolerances.

For 2025 Figure 6, job 124662568 evaluated 901 nitrogen states per EOS at 3.98 MPa,
100-325 K in 0.25 K steps. Density RMS errors against current NIST are 2.850% SRK
and 3.284% PR. PR Cp RMS error is 4.455%, maximum 28.870% at 129.75 K. The panel
layout and zoom windows match the paper; no quantitative error against digitized
2025 curves is claimed. These are model-versus-NIST errors.

The separate strict 1e-6 inversion criterion failed 873/901 SRK and 901/901 PR
states. The job therefore ended FAILED (3:0), despite finite positive exact-state
properties. Runtime was 50 seconds with 2 CPUs. Keep this failed criterion visible.
Source: 58cee1b4818ffaa4bdb5cadb0f590047db78263d; library build job 124657926.
Nitrogen evidence archive SHA-256:
`c09f88723a88122b5ebb6de331afff425577019996548b8b2e650ae690476d08`.

NIST silently resampled the initial 901-point query to 601 points. That response
was rejected. Two smaller exact-grid queries were validated and merged; the
reference manifest records URLs and hashes. No silent grid interpolation is used.

The 2022 Figures 8-11 show only 1 microsecond of coarse startup. They do not
reproduce the paper's steady flames. Profiles use actual polyhedral cell centroids
and nearest-axis rows, not r=0: 1.665 mm at 1/100 atm, 0.416 mm at 200 atm.
Axes retain the paper's axial windows; some property ranges are adapted to avoid
hiding startup values. Markers identify actual samples. Figure 8 uses cellwise
color and interpolated startup velocity streamlines. No flame-speed or LES curve
can be inferred from these results. See the existing pilot record and batch plan.
