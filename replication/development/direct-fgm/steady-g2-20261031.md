## Model and initial state

The [steady closure](../../../src/combustionModels/steadyFlamelet/README.md)
transports mixture fraction Z and obtains composition from one fixed-chi table.
It then solves full-SRK absolute enthalpy. It transports no progress variable
and adds no formation heat twice. This is a laminar, delta-PDF diagnostic.
It does not reproduce the papers' turbulent LES/PDF treatment or ignition delay.

The physical table has 129 mixture-fraction nodes and 53 GRI30 species at
5.61 MPa, with 85 K oxygen and 288 K methane endpoints. Its SHA256 is
`0126e2c7eea1afa960a99fbf7817f10463d522a563a162a16b76e0cfd6259bc2`.
The chi=100000/s profile retains its failed strict steady-residual criterion.
Using it for an approximate diagnostic does not change that verdict.

The separately qualified 10000-cell wedge uses the paper-sized exit areas.
The 5 us pilot starts at time zero with uniform Z=0.2 mapped from that table.
Consequently, far-downstream temperature remains near 3487 K. Its 45 and 70 mm
radial profiles mainly measure the initial fill. These cannot be compared as
stationary means against Cavalieri Figure 6 or Sharma Figure 4b.

At the final step, the cumulative physical energy defect is -4.467e-5 J.
The assembled equation defect is 1.307e-9 J. Pressure/enthalpy staging explains
most of their difference; the smaller value must not replace the physical budget.
Mass defect is approximately -8.08e-17 kg. Rank-0 mean step cost is 2.115 s,
with 87.9% in energy work. These are measured startup costs, not long-run forecasts.
