# Author mechanism provenance

Victor Zhukov (2019), Data for: The impact of methane oxidation kinetics on a
rocket nozzle flow, Mendeley Data V1, DOI 10.17632/rzw5tdbxgs.1.
Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rzw5tdbxgs/1 . Dataset licence: CC BY 4.0.
Downloaded 11 November 2026 through the public dataset page. The package contains
Reduced_Methane_Kong.inp, therm_reduced_CH4_Kong.dat and tran_reduced_CH4_Kong.dat.
The mechanism cites Zhukov and Kong (2018), DOI 10.3184/146867818X15066862094914.

zk-author-2019.yaml is a Cantera 3.2.0 ck2yaml conversion of those three files,
without permissive parsing or parameter changes. Conversion and Cantera validation
passed: 23 species, 51 reaction entries. This is an identified author-deposited
version, not proof of byte identity to the file used by Sharma in Fluent.
