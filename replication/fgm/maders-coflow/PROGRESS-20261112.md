## Case and software

[Maders 2017](https://www.abcm.org.br/proceedings/view/COB17/0722) Fig. 4 is an
atmospheric reacting FGM coupling target. Its four panels compare radial T and
CO at heights 10 and 40 mm. Figures 5–6 subsequently use pure methane, with a
separate table and taller domain; the diluted table must not be reused for them.

The [original Verhoeven thesis](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/3577661/719415.pdf#page=44)
resolves radius/diameter labels: fuel radius 6 mm, outer radius 27.5 mm; diluted
fuel is 55/45 molar CH4/N2. Selected inlets are 298 K, 101325 Pa, parabolic fuel
Umax 0.23 m/s and uniform air 0.23 m/s. Dry air O2:N2=1:3.76 and the structured
40,000-cell wedge are explicit reconstructions. Maders uses about 100,000
unstructured cells. The unavailable original mesh and table are not claimed.

Spitfire computes steady unity-Le adiabatic flamelets in mixture-fraction space;
Cantera 3.2 supplies DRM19 thermodynamics/rates (21 species, 84 reactions).
Prescribed Peters dissipation is a model reconstruction. These flamelets are not
the paper's radial coflow solution. The CFD uses our reactingFoamSolveZ, ideal-gas
NASA thermodynamics, optional Smooke transport, stock OF8 Fourier unity-Le
transport, and buoyancy force/energy. It does not qualify real-fluid EOS physics.
C=MW_H2O*sum(Yi/MWi), for CO2/H2O/H2, is dimensionless; its signed source is
kg/m3/s. Absolute enthalpy is initialized using the selected runtime thermo.
